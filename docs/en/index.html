From sketches to a product mark

  1. Shape

    Compare monochrome forms and record why each was kept or dropped.

  2. Color

    Keep the outline while trying colors on light and dark backgrounds.

  3. Review

    Choose options and write feedback in the local workbench.

  4. Deliver

    Check transparency and small sizes, then export SVGs, color specs, and the review page.
